Traffic validity check

Search Console is Google's own record of clicks from its results, so it cannot be inflated by a forged referrer. Comparing it with the sessions GA4 labels google / organic for the same page and window exposes traffic that only claims to come from Google. Genuine pages land within about 1.3×; anything at 3× or above is flagged.

Page GA4 google / organic GSC clicks Ratio

The same two pages are still carrying traffic that Search Console never saw. /blog/ai-prompts-for-photoshoot jumped from about 4 organic sessions a day to 160–200 on Oct 1 and stayed there until Oct 7; 92% of its google / organic sessions are desktop and only a third are engaged. /tools/ai-ugc-video-generator has run at about 9× since Sep 21. Removing both pages, organic sessions went 1,023 → 950 (−7%), not +62%.

What this report can't tell you yet

  • Technical SEO — Core Web Vitals, crawl errors, broken links. Needs PageSpeed Insights / Screaming Frog.
  • Backlink profile — referring domains, authority, toxic links. Needs Ahrefs / SEMrush / Moz.
  • True SERP rank tracking — GSC gives averaged position, not a daily rank per keyword.
  • Competitor benchmark — no comparison source connected yet.
  • On-page content audit — title/meta/heading structure, keyword cannibalization across pages.
